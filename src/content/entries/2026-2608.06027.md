## What they found

Component-level accuracy did not predict end-to-end form completion. The rule-based layer recovered many extraction errors, helping smaller, cheaper models match or surpass frontier models. Form completion dropped by up to 41 percentage points with real-speech transcripts. No single model was best across accuracy, cost, and latency.
