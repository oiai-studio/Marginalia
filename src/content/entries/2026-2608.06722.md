## What they found

CustomDance, a three-stage interactive system for 3D dance generation, improved authoring efficiency and dance quality over baselines. In a 25-participant ablation, it reduced authoring time from 28.28 to 11.66 minutes and clip replacements from 20.12 to 5.32, and eliminated detected artifacts. In a 25-participant ranking study, it was preferred most often for music alignment, description fulfillment, and overall performance. In a 15-participant long-sequence study, it required less authoring time than scratch choreography and received higher expert ratings than Lodge and MEGADance.
