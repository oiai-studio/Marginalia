## What they found

The three-stage interactive system improved authoring efficiency and dance quality. Ablations showed each stage contributed: motif planning improved structure and description fulfillment, retrieval reduced browsing effort, and the diagnoser reduced artifacts. CustomDance outperformed baselines in subjective rankings and objective metrics.
