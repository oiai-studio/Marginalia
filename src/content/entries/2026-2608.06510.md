## What they found

The paper argued that individual benefits and collective contestation capacity can move in opposite directions, as seen in spam filtering where inbox quality improved while organized challenge collapsed. It identified three conditions for sustained contestation: public observability, independent infrastructure, and governance that sustains contestation.
