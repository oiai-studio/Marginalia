## What they found

The paper argued that durable user empowerment depends on collective capacity to contest choices about agents, which was sustained where intermediary institutions provided adversarial governance and independent infrastructure, and collapsed where proprietary infrastructure and gated standard-setting foreclosed participation. It applied this to agentic AI, warning that current governance arrangements risk reproducing foreclosure patterns.
