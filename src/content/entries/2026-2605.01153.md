## What they found

The paper proposed a three-part design framework (multimodal alignment, real-time explainability, user agency) for human-AI interaction, illustrated with two hypothetical scenarios, without empirical testing, and acknowledged unresolved gesture-gaze conflicts and untested effects under time pressure.
