## What they found

The paper proposed a framework for an artificial singer that enters the collective dynamics of unconducted vocal ensembles, treating non-isochrony as a hard case. It described VocalLanes, a rehearsal tool that records and aligns singer-dominant phone recordings, and outlined stages for representation, state inference, and agent evaluation.
