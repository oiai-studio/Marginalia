## What they found

Proposed a framework for an artificial singer entering collective dynamics of unconducted vocal ensembles, treating non-isochrony as a hard case. Described VocalLanes, a rehearsal tool that records and aligns singer-dominant phone recordings, outlining stages for representation and state inference.
