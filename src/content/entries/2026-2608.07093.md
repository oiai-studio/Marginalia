## What they found

Uncertainty-aware visualizations preserved linguistic uncertainty in text-to-chart generation. Chart-to-text matching achieved 85% accuracy and text-to-chart 76%. They trended toward lower cognitive demand and 75% of participants preferred them, though line chart encodings required redesign.
