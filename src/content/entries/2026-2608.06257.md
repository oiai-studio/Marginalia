## What they found

MASS disentangled world dynamics from view rendering using a learned typed state, achieving higher state accuracy and lower cross-view inconsistency than video-based baselines on a matched Snake benchmark. It advanced worlds with 1,024 concurrent players for 10,000 recurrent steps.
