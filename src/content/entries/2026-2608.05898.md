## What they found

Analysis of 23 syllabi showed courses commonly require AI use, programming, and collaboration, with project work central. Topics emphasized AI/ML fundamentals, agentic development, prompting, and evaluation. Tools were rarely named; Claude Code, Cursor, and GitHub Copilot appeared most often.
