## What they found

Area chairs varied widely in engagement, from hands-off deference to reviewer consensus to hands-on advocacy for promising work. They viewed AI support with cautious optimism, valuing its potential to reduce cognitive load while fearing bias amplification, misinterpretation, and over-reliance.
