## What they found

The paper proposed PHOENIX, a system for autonomous satellite fault management using an onboard fine-tuned SLM and ground multi-agent LLM team. It reported simulated results: a 62% cache hit rate and 98.2% data suppression, but no empirical evaluation of the full system was conducted.
