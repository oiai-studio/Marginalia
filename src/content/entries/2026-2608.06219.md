## What they found

The touchscreen interface reduced completion time by 53.5% and improved sinusoidal path coverage from 84.1% to 90.7% compared with the joystick. NASA-TLX scores fell from 52 to 43, and the autonomous mode scored 31. Trust was slightly higher for the joystick than the touchscreen.
