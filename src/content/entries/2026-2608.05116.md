## What they found

Analysed issue reports and forum discussions from five AI developer tools, identifying three recurring visual accessibility barriers: screen-reader/assistive-technology problems, presentation and contrast issues, and readability and scaling limitations. Distribution varied by tool, as did maintainer response and closure patterns.
