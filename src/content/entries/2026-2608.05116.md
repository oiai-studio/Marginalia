## What they found

The study identified three recurring categories of visual accessibility barriers in AI developer tools: screen-reader and assistive-technology issues, visual presentation and contrast problems, and readability and scaling limitations. The distribution of these barriers varied across ecosystems, reflecting differences in editor, terminal, chat, and agent interaction surfaces.
