## What they found

The paper analysed public issue reports and forum discussions from five AI developer tools and identified three recurring categories of visual accessibility barriers: screen-reader and assistive-technology problems, visual presentation and contrast issues, and readability and scaling limitations. The distribution of these barriers varied across tools, reflecting differences in their interaction surfaces. Response patterns also differed, with maintainer participation and closure processes varying across ecosystems.
