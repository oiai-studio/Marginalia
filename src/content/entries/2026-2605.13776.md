## What they found

Using an LLM assistant shortened idea-generation time and reduced creative insight moments, though it improved code correctness. LLM-generated solutions clustered into more similar approaches than human ones. Four collaboration styles emerged; delegating only implementation, not ideation, preserved creativity best.
