## What they found

The paper argued that ASR failures for low-resource and non-standard varieties are structural linguistic policies reproducing colonial hierarchies. It proposed the 3M taxonomy (Misrecognition, Misalignment, Mistrust) and a participatory framework for culturally competent ASR, positioning communities as co-designers and auditors.
