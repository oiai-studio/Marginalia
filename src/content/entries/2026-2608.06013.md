## What they found

OneEmo, a unified multimodal reasoning model, outperformed similarly sized baselines on most emotion perception, understanding, and interaction benchmarks, and achieved competitive results against larger commercial models. The Emo-Chord training strategy improved performance over curriculum SFT alone.
