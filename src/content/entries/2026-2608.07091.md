## What they found

The framework selected CAM as the highest-fidelity, lowest-cost configuration across all profiles. Stronger compression or sparsification increased SSIM stability but reduced fidelity. Pareto filtering retained 12 Clinical, 13 Balanced, and 16 TinyML configurations.
