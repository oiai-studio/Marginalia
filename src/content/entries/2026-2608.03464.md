## What they found

Proprietary models outperformed open-source models overall, with Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview leading proprietary and Kimi K2.5 leading open-source models. More specific instructions improved annotation quality; intent-level generation remained most challenging, and adding chart images yielded only limited gains.
